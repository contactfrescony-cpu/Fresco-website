FRESCO NY PRODUCT IMAGE FOLDERS
================================

Add product images to the matching folder:

  tshirts       T-shirts and long-sleeve tees
  polos         Polo shirts
  hoodies       Sweatshirts and hoodies
  shirts        Shirts and overshirts
  outerwear     Jackets and fleece outerwear
  denim         Jeans
  trousers      Chinos, joggers and trousers
  shorts        Shorts

Accepted formats: JPG, JPEG, PNG, WEBP and AVIF.
Recommended format: WEBP, 800 x 1000 pixels (4:5 portrait), under 150 KB per image.
Photos are shown in a 4:5 frame, so portrait product shots on a plain background work best.

Use simple unique filenames without spaces, for example:

  washed-black-tshirt-01.webp
  olive-hoodie-02.webp
  checked-overshirt-03.webp

The website scans these folders automatically. Do not edit the HTML when
adding images, and do not rename the category folders.
